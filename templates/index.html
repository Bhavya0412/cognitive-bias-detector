The four biases this tool detects

1

Confirmation Bias

Definition: Favoring information that supports existing beliefs while ignoring contradictory evidence.

"This method always works — critics are wrong."

2

Anchoring Bias

Definition: Relying too heavily on the first piece of information encountered in decision-making.

"The first price was $1000, so anything lower feels cheap."

3

Availability Heuristic

Definition: Judging the likelihood of an event by how easily examples come to mind.

"I just saw it on the news, so it must be common."

4

Framing Effect

Definition: Decisions influenced by how information is presented or worded.

"90% success rate sounds better than 10% failure rate."
